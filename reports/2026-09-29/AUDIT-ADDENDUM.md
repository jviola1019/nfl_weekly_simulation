# Audit addendum (2026-09-29)

New defects found during Phase 0 Task 3 (test-harness triage) and the Phase 0 final review, after the test suite began loading every `R/` module and failing on unapproved skips. None of them is in `reports/2026-09-28/AUDIT.md` (on `docs/overhaul-spec`, PR #191). IDs continue that audit's series. Each ID is cited by a `KNOWN-DEFECT` skip in `tests/testthat/`, and the phase that fixes the ID removes the skip.

Severity uses the same scale as the audit: **Critical** changes shown numbers or invalidates claims; **High** is wrong behavior or a false claim; **Med** is a risk or drift; **Low** is hygiene.

| M21 | High | Venue weather and dome flags are joined by stadium **name**, and historical dates are fetched from the **forecast** endpoint. | M22 | Critical | **Root cause of M18, and much wider.** Every adjustment to the expected scores is lost; the simulator's means are drives × points per drive (+ home field for the home team). The turnover prior reads `turnovers_home`/`turnovers_away`, which nflverse schedules do not have, so `to_pg` is NaN for every team and `to_adj_pts` is NaN. At the turnover join `mu_home = pmax(mu_home + home_to_adj, 0)` becomes NaN for every game. The "safety" block then rebuilds any non-finite mu as `exp_drives * exp_ppd (+ HFA_pts)`. That silently discards the GLMM prior (35–70% weight), QB, rest, current-week injuries (offence and defence), travel, division/conference, pressure, explosive plays, the whole red-zone/special-teams/location/third-down/turnover/penalty/situational/momentum block, and the environment/weather adjustments. **Observed 2026-09-29** (local run of 2024 week 15 with renv): the final `mu_home`/`mu_away` equal the rescue formula in **16 of 16** games (to 1e-9), and `home_to_adj`/`away_to_adj` are NA in 16 of 16. The fix is **not** a one-line coalesce: several lost terms have implausible sizes. For KC @ CLE the dropped situational block alone is −17.06 (CLE) / +12.49 (KC) points (home/away EPA split −17.56 × 0.7, special teams −5.04 × 0.7), a 29.6-point swing against a 4.5-point market spread. Reviving them untested would make the model far worse. Recommended: make the effective model explicit, then re-admit terms one at a time only if the walk-forward backtest shows they help. The Tune-window screen (`reports/2026-09-30/validation-sprint/`) found no situational feature with signal beyond the close. | `NFLsimulation.R:4449-4468` (`to_team` from absent columns), `:5125-5133` (NaN join), `:6271-6284` (rescue), `:5047-5052` (the dropped block); reproduction scripts in the session scratchpad (check_mu_rescue.R, kc_cle_terms.R) |

The schedule's own `home_rest`/`away_rest` columns are the point-in-time source. | `NFLsimulation.R:4077-4103` (`:4091` first-game date) |
